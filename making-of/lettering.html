Lettering

The six sound-effect words, from painted sprite to what the components draw. Each word was generated as solid black brush lettering on transparency, traced into one vector path, and is painted in code with an outline and a fill, so the same shape can be solid, hollow or screentone at any size.

wordgenerated spritetraced pathsolidhollowdotted

The sprites are in art/words/. tools/trace.py turns them into the paths in core.js, and SakugaFX.paint(name, { col, edge, dots }) paints one onto a canvas. The tracing drops specks of a few pixels and rounds the edges very slightly; at the sizes the words are shown, you cannot see the difference.
